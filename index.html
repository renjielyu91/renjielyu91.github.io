About me

I am a tenure-track assistant professor in the School of Mathematical Sciences at Xiamen University. I work in algebraic geometry, mainly studying algebraic varieties through Hodge theory. My research includes weak Torelli theorems, Chow groups of cubic hypersurfaces and related hyper-Kähler varieties, and degenerations of Hodge structures. I received my Ph.D. from the University of Amsterdam under the supervision of Mingmin Shen. Previously, I was a postdoc with Baohua Fu at the Morningside Center of Mathematics, CAS.
