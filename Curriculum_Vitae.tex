\vspace{2em}

  I am an assistant professor at the School of Mathematical Sciences,
  Xiamen University. I was a postdoctoral researcher with Baohua Fu at the
  Morningside Center of Mathematics, Chinese Academy of Sciences. I received
  my Ph.D. from the University of Amsterdam under the supervision of Mingmin Shen.

  My research is in algebraic geometry. I work on degeneration of Hodge structures,
  algebraic cycles, monodromy of hypersurface singularities, and automorphisms of algebraic varieties.
